Doo Doo Town - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Doo Doo Town ("the game") is made by Matthew Demore (No End Studios) for Mt. Washington Sewer & Drain. This policy covers the Android app and the web version.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your save game, settings, high scores and unlocked songs are saved only on your own phone or browser (local storage). They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data deletes them.

Permissions

The Android app does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos or files. The game files are built into the app, so it works without an internet connection.

Children

The game is meant for players 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The Android app is distributed through Google Play, which is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
